# Stein SDK: build mods for Stein Loader (experimental API)

The first public version of the kit for mod developers. It is **experimental**: it works, and it is what our own mods use, but anything marked `@Experimental` may still change between Stein Loader versions.

- **Java 25, readable names**: `setup` builds the game with MCP names on your machine, and `build` compiles, checks and packs the `.steinmod` — no Gradle, nothing from Mojang in the package.
- **Events and services**: HUD on the grid, Panel pages (with sliders and key pickers), inventory through real clicks, raycast and attacks, entity damage and health, chat, sounds, networking and more.
- **A complete example mod** (Damage Indicator) and a template to start from.
- **MIT licence** for the API, the template and the examples.

Needs Stein Loader 0.1.22 or newer.
